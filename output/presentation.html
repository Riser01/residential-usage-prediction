SLIDE 01 • BUSINESS CONTEXT & DELIVERABLES

Solving Shared Amenity Friction with Predictive Intelligence

Residential communities manage high-demand shared amenities: Gym, Swimming Pool, Badminton, Tennis, Clubhouse, and Multipurpose Hall. Traditional static scheduling creates peak crowding and off-peak vacancy.

The 4 Core Predictions:

  • Facility: Which amenity will the resident book next?
  • Usage Day: Which day of the week (Mon–Sun)?
  • Usage Time: What operating hour (e.g. 07:00, 18:00)?
  • Notification Timing: When to send a proactive nudge to prompt booking?

SLIDE 03 • MODELING ARCHITECTURE (§3.2)

Cascaded Multi-Target GBDT Pipeline

Rather than 4 disconnected models that output incompatible combinations, our architecture uses a cascaded, hierarchical LightGBM pipeline:

  1. Stage 1 (Facility Classifier): Evaluates user preferences, recency, and Bayes shrinkage priors.
  2. Stage 2 (Usage Day Classifier): Predicts day conditioned on user history and predicted facility.
  3. Stage 3 (Usage Hour Classifier): Predicts operating hour slot (06..22) conditioned on facility and day context.
  4. Stage 4 (Lead Time Quantile Regressor, α=0.35): Optimizes for proactive nudge timing to guarantee alerts precede bookings.
Leakage Control Highlights
  • Strict chronological train/val/test splitting (Month 6 unseen holdout).
  • Expanding-window causal state tracker (0 future lookahead).
  • Automated temporal assertion tests (tests/test_leakage_safety.py).
  • Fast CPU inference (<10ms per prediction).
